## Problem Description

**Second roles.**
- `docs/guide.md` §1, item 4 sets the rule for a role that shares its device's connection and lock. The role can be opened on its own, which opens the device if it's closed. Closing the role closes the device only if the role opened it. Closing the device closes the role.
- That rule is implemented by hand twice, in `Foldio360Halo` (`stages/foldio360.py:1013-1061`) and `GPhoto2FocusDrive` (`cameras/gphoto2.py:550-607`). Each copies the parent's `_lock`, keeps a flag for whether it opened the parent, and has the parent clear its open state through `_turntable_closed` or `_detach`.
- The two disagree on something the guide doesn't say: whether opening the device opens the role. Opening the camera opens the focus drive (`_attach`). Opening the turntable leaves the halo closed.

**Foldio transport.**
- The transport is typed `Any` (`foldio360.py:500`). Its seven members (connect, close, send, read, drain, connected, address) are implicit, and nothing checks that `FakeNusTransport` matches the real transport.

**Axis arithmetic.**
- The shortest signed offset on a wrapping axis is computed in `foldio360.py:592`, `simulated.py:104` and `_angle_between` (`foldio360.py:1078`).
- The simulated stage's jog re-implements limit clamping (`simulated.py:172-175`).

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `device.py` provides the second-role mechanism: the shared lock and the open rule. A second role is open whenever its device is (as long as the hardware offers it). It can also be opened on its own, which opens the device, and closing it then closes the device too, but only in that case. Closing the device closes the role.
- [ ] `Foldio360Halo` and `GPhoto2FocusDrive` both use it. The halo becomes open when the turntable opens, and `halo.open()` still works on a closed turntable. Tests assert the open rule for both.
- [ ] A `FoldioTransport` `Protocol` types the seam. `NusTransport` and `FakeNusTransport` are both checked against it.
- [ ] `Axis.shortest_offset()` and `Axis.clamp()` exist with tests, and Foldio360 and the simulated stage use them.
- [ ] The base `_move_by` keeps its leg-splitting. X-Carve uses the base `_move_by`, and the leg-splitting is right for any future stage that has a wrapping axis but moves only to absolute positions.
- [ ] `docs/guide.md` §1, item 4 adds that opening the device opens its second roles. The halo's docstring and `docs/hardware/` page say the same.
