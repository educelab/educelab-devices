# Specification: XLC Lights and X-Carve Stage

**Track ID:** xlc-xcarve_20261012
**Type:** Feature
**Created:** 2026-10-12
**Status:** Draft

## Summary

Port the XLC1 and XLC2 light controllers to `Light` drivers and the X-Carve gantry (GRBL 1.1g) to a `Stage` driver, from `pgs-scan` `dev`, fixing the known serial and motion defects, and bring the XLC firmware into this repo, updated to acknowledge every command.

## Context

On `pgs-scan` `dev`, `XLC1`/`XLC2` (`pgs_scan/components/lights.py`) sit on a `SerialObject(QObject)` base whose port search accepts any one matching criterion (#84) and shares a class-level read buffer. `XController` (`pgs_scan/components/motion_stages.py`) homes during connect, polls on Qt timers, and its blocking move can wait forever when the target is clamped (#71, no timeout per #67). The serial transport from the MISHA track is reused.

## User Story

As the `pgs-scan` developer, I want the gantry and its lights behind the `Stage` and `Light` interfaces so that the scanner can drop its Qt-coupled device classes and their known bugs.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] `XLC1` (`lights.xlc`): handshake `<?>` → `EduceLab XLC1 (FW x.y)`; one RGB channel `"rgb"`; `set_color` → `<C,r,g,b>`; `set_level` scales a grey value.
- [ ] `XLC2` (`lights.xlc`): handshake matching `EduceLab XLC2 (FW x.y.z)`; channels `"white"` (`<B,n>`) and `"940nm"` (`<X,n>`, wavelength metadata 940); levels scaled to the firmware's brightness range.
- [ ] XLC1 and XLC2 firmware updated (versions bumped): every command is answered with exactly one line, `OK` or `ERR <reason>`; debug prints (`Got a brightness command`, …) removed; handshake strings unchanged. Drivers wait for `OK`, raise `CommandRejected` on `ERR`, and refuse older firmware with a `DeviceError` pointing to the reflash steps.
- [ ] Port matching requires all given criteria (no #84); no shared mutable buffers between instances.
- [ ] `XCarve` (`stages.xcarve`): GRBL banner on open, **no homing on open**; `requires_homing` true and `home()` runs `$H` with a timeout; moves before homing raise.
- [ ] Axes `"x"`, `"y"`, `"z"` (linear, mm, machine coordinates) with limits passed in by the consumer; out-of-limit targets raise instead of clamping (fixes #71).
- [ ] `move_to`/`move_by` block until GRBL reports `Idle` at the target within tolerance, with a default timeout from distance and feed rate (fixes #67); `jog` uses `$J` and returns immediately; `stop()` sends jog-cancel/feed-hold real-time bytes and is callable during a move.
- [ ] `position()` is served from status reports polled internally during moves, so it is live for GRBL; alarms and unexpected disconnects are delivered to listeners and raise on the next call.
- [ ] Feed rates are constructor/settings parameters, not hidden constants.
- [ ] Contract suites pass on scripted fake transports (XLC protocol, GRBL protocol).
- [ ] Firmware copied to `firmware/xlc1/` and `firmware/xlc2/` unchanged first, with provenance in the commit message, then updated.
- [ ] `docs/hardware/xlc.md` and `docs/hardware/xcarve-grbl.md`.
- [ ] `el-devices xlc` and `el-devices xcarve` subcommands.

## Technical Notes

- Confirm XLC2's brightness range from `hardware/xlight/XLC2/XLC2_firmware/XLC2_firmware.ino`.
- **Reflashing risk:** `pgs-scan` `dev` talks to XLC boards today. Check whether its `XLC1`/`XLC2` classes tolerate the new reply lines; if not, reflash a rig only together with that rig's `pgs-scan` migration to these drivers.
- `0x1A86` is the generic CH340 VID; XLC2 discovery must rely on the handshake, not VID alone.
- GRBL real-time commands (`?`, `0x85`, `!`) bypass the line buffer and are the only safe way to query or interrupt during a move.
